"Other options": **dark, badass animal humanoids** (gorilla, elephant, croc, falcon/eagle, rabbit, and so on), so armor and weapons make sense. Where these fit (evolutions vs other species) is being clarified.
- A1 follow-up: **(b)** George's Skerbie stays a dark psychic critter at every stage, growing bigger and more armored. **The animal humanoids are other species for the later team feature** (gorilla, croc, falcon, rabbit, elephant...), each with its own **type aptitude**, which also solves the "team all same type" problem. Not in v1 art.
- A2: Detailed 16-bit pixel art (late-SNES / modern indie). Scene canvas **225×487 px at exactly 5×** = 1125×2436 (iPhone X native). Skerbie: baby 32×32, kid 48×48, adult 64×64. Dark outlines on characters, soft or no outlines on backgrounds. About 12–16 colors per character, about 32 for the world. Landscape mode: being decided.
- A2 follow-up: **Landscape only where it makes sense = Jungle Run** (487×225 canvas at 5×). Everything else is portrait only (225×487). (George briefly wanted both everywhere, then reverted.)
- A3: **Dark-but-cozy fantasy**: shadowy forests and caves outside, a lantern-lit den inside. World palette: deep charcoal, slate blue, mossy dark green. Warm amber/orange from lanterns, fire and the hearth. **Red is reserved for his eyes, danger (raiders) and fire.** Vivid element colors: fire orange, water teal, lightning yellow-white, earth brown-green, ice pale blue.
- A3 follow-up (**must be in the art request**): Signature effect = **smoke**. Red eyes glow faintly, stronger at night and in caves, so he reads even as a silhouette. Dark smoke wisps curl off him when excited, powering up, evolving or moving fast (a smoky trail in Jungle Run). On big moves the smoke flares and tints toward the element or type in use. Built as a reusable effect.
- A4: Home = a **den hollowed out under the roots of a huge ancient tree** at the edge of a dark forest, as a **cutaway side view**. Top ~30% **surface**: forest floor, time-of-day sky, the trunk rising out of frame, a root door; the garden, merchant cart and overflow pile live here. Middle ~50% **den**: root-and-stone walls, hearth, lanterns, bed, pantry, chalkboard, blueprint board, chest; buildables fill in here. Bottom ~20% **deep roots**: dark roots and stone; at the kid stage **a crack splits the floor = the Cave Delve entrance**, glowing faintly from below.
- A4 follow-up: The den levels up in **3 visible tiers**: **Burrow** (rough dirt hollow, leaf-pile bed, one candle, bare roots), **Hideout** (stone-lined walls, real hearth, hanging lanterns, root-carved shelves, rug), **Lair** (carved stone pillars, iron fixtures, trophy mounts, banners, rune-lit wall glyphs, iron-reinforced root door). Every buildable has a fixed spot in the den and is drawn in all 3 tier styles. (Game spec: what triggers each tier is still open. Assumed to be a blueprint build, level-gated.)
- A5 Forms: **Egg**: smooth charcoal-black shell, faint red ember-glow cracks, smoke wisps leak near hatching. **Baby (32px)**: small and round, oversized head and ears, soft black fuzz, huge smoky red eyes, stub tail; clumsy, adorable, a little spooky. **Kids (48px)**, leaner, longer limbs: *Speed* sleek, swept-back ears, long thin tail, tattered trailing scarf; *Strength* stockier, thick grey fur mane on shoulders, wrapped fists; *Mind* extra-tall ears with smoky glowing tips, faint rune markings, hovers slightly. **Adults (64px)**: *Speed = Shadow Stalker* (lean, hooded cowl, blade-tipped tail, constant smoke trail); *Strength = Juggernaut* (hulking, scarred grey fur, heavy grounded stance); *Mind = Occultist* (robed, floating, glowing fur sigils, smoke halo); *Balanced = Warden* (a mix; cloaked, steady, watchful). **Armor and weapons = gear overlays** on top of the body, so any form can wear any gear. - A7 Dye zones (**word it exactly like this in the order**): 4 zones: (1) **main fur** (default black), (2) **accent fur**: belly, mane, ear tips, markings (default dark grey), (3) **eye glow** (default red), (4) **smoke**, a bonus zone unlocked by a dye pack. Each zone is drawn with its **own reserved set of 4 shades** (highlight, base, shadow, deep shadow) used nowhere else on the sprite. The game generates a matching 4-shade ramp from any chosen hex color and swaps it in, so shading and depth survive any color. **Outlines and gear are never dyed** (gear dyeing comes later).
- A7 follow-up: The player can **change colors freely, any time**, from the baby stage on, and colors carry through evolutions. The egg is always the default look. All 7 bodies use the same 4 zone ramps in consistent positions.
- A8 Lighting: 4 real-clock phases: **Dawn** (6–9am, cool pinkish-grey, mist), **Day** (9am–5pm, muted overcast, never bright sunny), **Dusk** (5–8pm, deep orange-to-purple, long shadows, lanterns flicker on), **Night** (8pm–6am, dark blue, warm lantern pools in the den, fireflies on the surface, his eyes glowing brightest). Drawn as neutral-lit backgrounds + **4 sky paintings + 4 lighting overlays** (tint + glow maps for lanterns and hearth), blended smoothly. The den changes less than the surface. **Overall feel: lo-fi beats art colors.**
- A8 follow-up: **Lo-fi treatment**: soft contrast; shadows lean dusky purple and deep teal, never pure black (**pure black is reserved for his fur**); warm glowy highlights (soft pink, peach, amber, like a 2am desk lamp); subtle light grain or dither overlay that keeps pixels crisp; ambient weather (gentle rain some nights with streaks, drips at the door and a cozier hearth; drifting leaves by day; no gameplay effect). **Music: lo-fi music** (George said lo-fi music, not specifically chiptune; sourcing queued as A20).
